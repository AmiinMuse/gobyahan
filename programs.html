Our Programs

Signature initiatives building skills, confidence, and community.

Gobyahan's programs are designed to meet women and young people where they are — providing training, mentorship, and platforms to amplify their voices.

Filmmaking Workshops

Hands-on training in documentary and narrative filmmaking, taught by working professionals. Participants leave with a finished short film and an industry network.

Youth Media Lab

An after-school program for teenagers to learn journalism, podcasting, and digital storytelling — and to publish work that reaches their communities.

Women Leaders Initiative

Mentorship, leadership training, and grant funding for women launching media and advocacy projects in their communities.

Community Screenings

Public screenings and conversations that bring our participants' work to wider audiences and spark dialogue on issues that matter.

Advocacy & Policy

Working with partners to advance policies that protect and expand opportunities for women and youth in media and public life.

Scholarships & Grants

Direct financial support for participants pursuing further training, education, or independent projects in storytelling and advocacy.

Want to Bring a Program to Your Community?

We partner with schools, community centers, and organizations to bring Gobyahan's programs to new places.
