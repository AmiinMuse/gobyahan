Who We Are

Gobyahan is a nonprofit dedicated to creating opportunities for underrepresented voices to be heard. Through media, mentorship, and meaningful action, we partner with communities to drive lasting change.

Storytelling

We train aspiring filmmakers and journalists to share stories that matter — stories rooted in their own communities and lived experiences.

Education

Our workshops and mentorship programs equip young people with the skills, confidence, and networks they need to thrive.

Advocacy

We work alongside partners to advocate for the rights, dignity, and representation of women and youth across our communities.

Our Mission

To empower women and young people by providing them with the tools, training, and platforms to tell their own stories and shape their own futures.

We believe that when voices are heard — truly heard — communities transform.
