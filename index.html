Who We Are

Gobyahan is a non-profit storytelling platform dedicated to amplifying Somali stories about climate change, lived experiences, and resilience. It uses film and multimedia storytelling to document lived realities, preserve cultural memory, and center voices often left out of global climate and development conversations.

Storytelling

We train young Somali filmmakers and multimedia storytellers to share stories rooted in their own communities and lived experiences.

Climate Awareness

We document how drought, environmental change, and shifting livelihoods are reshaping Somali communities and pastoral traditions.

Cultural Preservation

We safeguard oral histories, poetry, songs, and traditional knowledge tied to the land — before they are lost.
