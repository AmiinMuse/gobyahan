About Us

Our story, our mission, and the people behind Gobyahan.

Who We Are

Gobyahan is a nonprofit organization founded to create space for women and young people to tell their own stories. Through filmmaking, journalism, and community-led media, we equip emerging voices with the tools and platforms they need to share what matters most to them.

Since our founding, we have worked alongside community partners, educators, and artists to build programs that center dignity, representation, and impact.

Our Story

Our Mission

To empower women and young people by providing them with the tools, training, and platforms to tell their own stories and shape their own futures. We believe that representation and voice are essential to building stronger, more equitable communities.

Our Vision

A world in which every woman and young person — regardless of background — has the opportunity to be heard, to lead, and to inspire change in their community and beyond.
