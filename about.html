About Us

Our story, our mission, and the people behind Gobyahan.

Our Story

Somaliland has a rich storytelling tradition rooted in oral history and nomadic pastoralism, both of which have preserved knowledge and identity for generations. Yet few organizations focus specifically on youth-led filmmaking, and even fewer operate at the intersection of storytelling, climate awareness, and cultural preservation.

At the same time, climate change poses one of the greatest threats to Somaliland's way of life. Recurrent droughts, environmental degradation, displacement, and changing pastoral livelihoods are reshaping communities — and the stories, oral histories, poetry, songs, and traditional knowledge tied to the land are at risk of being lost.

Gobyahan was created to bridge these gaps by empowering young storytellers to document their communities, preserve cultural heritage, and raise awareness about the environmental and social challenges shaping Somaliland's future.

Our Story

Our Mission

Gobyahan is a hybrid storytelling and film production platform that empowers Somali storyteller youth through training, workshops, and collaborative production. We create films, documentaries, and multimedia projects that center Somali voices and lived experiences, with a focus on climate change and cultural memory.

Our Vision

A future where Somali voices are leading the global narrative on climate change, culture, and resilience through their own storytelling — a thriving creative ecosystem where Somali youth are not only documenters of their realities, but active shapers of how their histories, struggles, and futures are understood.

Leadership & Board

Gobyahan is led by Founder and Executive Director Nimco Yuusuf and governed by a board that provides strategic oversight and organizational guidance.
